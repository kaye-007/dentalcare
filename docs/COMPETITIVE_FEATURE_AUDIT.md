# DentalCare — capability audit

Date: 2026-09-28 · Companion to [APPLE_EXPERIENCE_PASS.md](./APPLE_EXPERIENCE_PASS.md)

**What this is.** A check of DentalCare's capabilities against the list a comparable product, CRMDent, advertises on its public page (crmdent.com, English version, read 2026-09-28). The page was used as a list of capabilities a dental clinic might expect, nothing more:

- its design and information architecture were not looked at for reuse;
- nothing was added because it appears there;
- no ranking of products is made, here or in the strategy section.

Each capability is judged by what an Albanian clinic needs and what DentalCare does today, verified in the code and on the running demo, not from memory.

**The scale.**

| Class | Meaning                                           |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **A** | DentalCare already does this well                 |
| **B** | DentalCare has it, but the experience can improve |
| **C** | Partial: part of the capability exists            |
| **D** | A meaningful capability that is missing           |
| **E** | Not worth adding now, with the reason             |

Where this pass changed a class, the row says so ("was C").

## 2. Where DentalCare already goes further for an Albanian clinic

These are not on the reference list and matter here:

- **Fiscalization.** Invoices are registered with the tax authority, with the queue, the 48-hour limit, the certificate upload and plain status words.
- **TVSH as the law applies it.** Cosmetic work carries it and medical work is exempt, per service, and the TVSH report says what was charged.
- **Lek with euro alongside.** Books are kept in lek, and estimates for patients from abroad are also shown in euro.
- **Albanian names found however typed.** A missing ë or ç, or the surname typed first, still finds the patient ("cela era" finds Era Çela).
- **Undo instead of "Are you sure?"** It applies to moving, check-in, lab steps (Fitted included, for ten minutes) and cancelled lab work.
- **The cash drawer's count and approval**, rather than a running total.

## 4. Why would an Albanian clinic switch to DentalCare?

The answer is product strategy, not a comparison.

**1. It is built for how an Albanian clinic actually works.** A clinic here needs:

- fiscal invoices registered with the tax authority, and TVSH only on cosmetic work;
- books in lek, with euro for the patient from abroad;
- messages that reach patients on WhatsApp and Viber, in Albanian;
- names with ë and ç found however someone types them.

DentalCare does these out of the box. A new clinic opens with Tirana's time zone, lek, +355 phones and Albanian patient messages, already set.

**2. The money is right, and it can prove it.** Every piece of charted work can become a bill without retyping it. Every payment issues its receipt. The cash drawer closes with a count, and any difference waits for a manager. Outstanding equals the sum of what patients owe, and the demo seed refuses to load if it doesn't. Reports count on the clinic's calendar, and the accountant gets an Excel file.

**3. Simple on the surface, complete underneath.** Each screen has one obvious next step, and Undo replaces "Are you sure?". The dashboard opens on what needs attention. Nothing was removed to get there: the lab, suppliers, fiscal queue, perio chart and permissions are all still one step away.

**4. The whole clinic is connected.** The chart feeds the invoice, the invoice the payment, the payment the receipt and the books. Lab work belongs to the patient and shows up when they are in the chair. Stock knows who it is bought from. A patient due for a check-up is one tap from an invitation in Albanian.

**5. Moving in is an afternoon, not a project.** Upload the Excel list the clinic already keeps, and DentalCare reads it: "250 patients found — Import". Balances come across, duplicates are caught, and nothing is saved until the owner says so.

**6. It works on the phone the dentist already has.** The working screens are checked at phone widths, down to 320 px, for every role.

**Where a clinic might still hesitate** (and what the roadmap says):

- several branches;
- an evening email summary;
- online booking;
- AI imaging.

Each is a deliberate "not yet" with a stated trigger, in §3.
