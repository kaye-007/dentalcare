# DentalCare — CRM backlog

Accepted 2026-09-28 · Evidence and reasoning: [ALBANIAN_CRM_FINAL_AUDIT.md](./ALBANIAN_CRM_FINAL_AUDIT.md)

**Status: on hold.** Security first. No item here starts until the security program is complete and the repository is secure enough for the next development phase. Then the items are built in this order, starting with P1.

**The principle.** Keep the capability, remove the friction. Nothing is added only to match CRMDent.

## P1

1. **Dentist Today.** A checked-in patient stays in the active/waiting workflow until the visit is actually completed.
2. **Treatment plans connected to the real workflow:** plan line → booking → visit or clinical completion → billing. Add the missing automated test for invoicing completed plan work.
3. **The mobile treatment plan.** All the important information is reachable without horizontal scrolling.

## P2

4. **The estimate flow:** Give to patient → Accepted / Declined → Book first visit. The estimate can be sent through WhatsApp. Kept extremely simple.
5. **Current treatment on the patient overview,** with the related attention items visible.

## P3

6. **Tomorrow's reminders,** reviewed through the clinic's WhatsApp Business account.
7. **The cash drawer's arithmetic:** starting balance → cash in/out → expected closing balance → actual → difference.
8. **After closing time,** the visits that remain incomplete.
9. **Lab work ready:** the clinic can notify the patient.

## P4

10. **Consider** importing future appointments during migration.
11. **Imaging:** a practical X-ray viewer (zoom and contrast, tooth association).

## Not to be added

- team chat;
- voice and video;
- AI X-ray analysis;
- lab technician portal;
- multiple branches;
- recurring appointment series;
- purchase orders;
- a separate estimates module.
